# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openpressurecooker

**Short description:** Learn why a pressure cooker is faster: pressure, boiling point, heating energy and cooking rate, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openpressurecooker is a beginner-friendly learning repo that explains why a pressure cooker cooks faster. In three short sessions you go from the simple idea that higher pressure means a higher boiling point, to the Clausius-Clapeyron relation and a valve weight design, to a time-stepping simulation of heating, venting and cooking that you validate against an energy balance and steam-table values. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. It is education only: never modify or block the valves of real equipment.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
