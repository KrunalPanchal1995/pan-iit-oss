# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opencentripetal

**Short description:** Learn why cars slide on bends and how banking fixes it: centripetal force, friction and a validated simulation, in 4 sessions.

**Full description:** opencentripetal is a beginner-friendly learning repo about circular motion. In four short sessions you go from the idea that a bend needs an inward push (and that the feeling of being thrown outward is only inertia), to the equations for friction on a flat curve and for a banked curve with its safe speed range, to a time-stepping simulation of a car whose tyres obey a friction circle. The simulation is checked against the formulas, against energy conservation, against the roller-coaster-loop minimum speed, and to a transition spiral that builds the sideways acceleration up gradually. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as load transfer, rollover and lap-time planning. Educational models only; this is not a road design tool.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
