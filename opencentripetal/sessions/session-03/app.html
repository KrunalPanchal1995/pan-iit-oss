Session 3: the friction circle and the grip limit

What the driver asks of the tyres against what the road can give; the graph shows the grip limit against radius. A third view, "Slide or tip?", adds the track width and the height of the centre of gravity.

This is a simplified educational model. It is not a validated design tool and must not be used for real structures, electrical installations, vehicles, industrial processes or medical decisions.
