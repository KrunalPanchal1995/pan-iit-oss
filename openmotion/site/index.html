openmotion

Read position, velocity and acceleration graphs like a physicist, in three sessions.

  • Session 1: One motion, three graphs: slope and area (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Equations of motion, piecewise trips and stopping distance (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: Differentiating and integrating noisy data (Level 3, 90 min)
