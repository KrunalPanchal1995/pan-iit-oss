# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openmotion

**Short description:** Read position, velocity and acceleration graphs of a moving body, from a car on a road to noisy phone data, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openmotion is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the three graphs of motion. In three short sessions you go from a car with adjustable acceleration and its linked position, velocity and acceleration graphs (what slope and area mean), to the equations of motion, piecewise trips and a stopping-distance design problem, to numerical differentiation and integration of noisy position data that you validate against an exact motion. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as automatic smoothing, error bars and tracking a real phone video. The models are simplified and are for learning, not for judging real road safety.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
