# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opensymbolic

**Short description:** Learn symbolic regression: expression trees, complexity versus error, and a real formula search in plain numpy.

**Full description:** opensymbolic is a beginner-friendly learning repo about finding formulas from data. In three short sessions you go from a falling ball and a short list of candidate formulas, to expression trees whose size and error you can count and plot (the Pareto trade-off between simple and accurate), to a real search that enumerates every small formula, fits its constants exactly, and recovers the quadratic law, the pendulum law and the Arrhenius law from noisy synthetic data. You measure how often it succeeds, study how it fails (overfitting, a law outside the search space), and use units to throw out impossible formulas. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The idea is inspired by research that uses symbolic regression to find interpretable rate rules in combustion chemistry. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as genetic search, many inputs and weighted errors.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
