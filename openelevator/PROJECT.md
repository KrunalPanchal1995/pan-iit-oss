# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openelevator

**Short description:** Learn why lifts feel the way they do: motion profiles, counterweights, motor power and jerk, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openelevator is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the motion of a lift (elevator). In three short sessions you go from a speed-time graph and the reason you feel heavier when a lift speeds up, to the trapezoid and S-curve motion profiles, travel time, comfort limits and the motor power with a counterweight, and finally to a time-stepping simulation of a cable-counterweight system with a motor force limit. The simulation is checked against the exact travel-time formula and against an energy balance, including energy fed back to the grid by a heavy car going down. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. It is an educational model only, not a lift design tool: real lifts are designed to safety standards by qualified engineers.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
