# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openstressstrain

**Short description:** Learn stress, strain, Hooke's law and yield, and choose a safe material and size for a hanging load, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openstressstrain is a beginner-friendly learning repo about how materials behave when they are pulled. In three short sessions you go from stress and strain of a simple bar and the shape of its curve (elastic versus plastic), to Hooke's law, Young's modulus, yield strength and the factor of safety in a small design problem that chooses a material and a diameter, to a one-dimensional elastic-plastic simulation with strain hardening, loading and unloading, the 0.2 percent offset yield and a sensitivity study checked against hand calculations. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All material numbers are round textbook-typical values for teaching; this is not a design tool. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as necking, curve fitting, material-selection charts and uncertainty.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
