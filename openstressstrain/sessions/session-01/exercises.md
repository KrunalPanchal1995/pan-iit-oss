# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** In `lesson.py` change the diameter to 6 mm and print the stress for 5 kN. Predict it first and say whether the bar is elastic.
2. **Stretch.** Write `extension_for(force_kn, d_mm)` that returns the extension in mm using `pull`, and use it to find the largest force (to 0.5 kN) that keeps a 10 mm bar elastic.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, using the words stress and strain, why a very long wire and a very short wire of the same metal and thickness have the same yield stress but different yield extensions.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
