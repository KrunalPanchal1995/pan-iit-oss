# Coding tasks: Session 3

1. **Easy.** Print GM for cargo heights 2.0, 3.0 and 3.8 m with 120 t. Predict the signs first, then check them against the figure.
2. **Stretch.** Compute the GZ curve for 160 t at 2.5 m and find the heel angle where the deck edge goes under and where GZ is largest.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why moving cargo sideways by 0.2 m heels the barge, and why the same shift heels it less when GM is larger.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
