# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openbuoyancy

**Short description:** Learn buoyancy, barge loading and ship stability (GM and the righting-arm curve) from scratch in 4 sessions.

**Full description:** openbuoyancy is a beginner-friendly learning repo about floating bodies. In four short sessions you go from the idea that a floating object pushes aside its own weight of water, to the buoyant force F = rho g V with barge draft, load capacity and balloon lift, to the stability of a floating box: centre of buoyancy, centre of gravity, metacentre and the righting-arm curve computed from exact submerged geometry and checked against the small-angle formula, and finally to the free-surface effect that lets liquid in a tank rob the barge of its stability. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The models are simplified teaching models, not naval architecture tools. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as round-bilge hulls, free-surface effects and rolling in waves.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
