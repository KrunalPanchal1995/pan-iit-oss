# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Change the altitude in `lesson.py` to 800 km. Predict the period before you run it (longer than 92.4 minutes), then check.
2. **Stretch.** Write `speed_ratio(r1, r2)` that gives v1 / v2 and show that doubling the radius cuts the speed by a factor of sqrt(2).
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, in your own words and with a sketch, why a cannonball fired at 60 percent of the circular speed hits the ground but one fired at 100 percent does not.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
