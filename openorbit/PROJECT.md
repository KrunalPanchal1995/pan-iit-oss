# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openorbit

**Short description:** Learn orbits and plan a Hohmann transfer with Kepler's laws, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openorbit is a beginner-friendly learning repo that teaches how satellites orbit and how to move them between orbits. In three short sessions you go from the picture of a satellite falling around the Earth and Kepler's three laws, to the equations of circular and elliptical orbits (vis-viva, Kepler's equation, two numerical integrators compared), to planning a Hohmann transfer between two circular orbits and checking it by integrating the trajectory. Along the way you compare it with a bi-elliptic transfer, price a plane change, solve a phasing problem and see where the instant-burn model fails. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as full rendezvous, low-thrust spirals and interplanetary transfers. Level: undergraduate, teaching model only, not for mission design.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
