# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openbeam

**Short description:** Learn beam loading and deflection: shear, moment, deflection and the lightest beam that passes, in 4 sessions.

**Full description:** openbeam is a beginner-friendly learning repo about beams, the horizontal members that carry floors, bridges and shelves. In four short sessions you go from the reactions, shear force and bending moment of a single point load, to the second moment of area, deflection and bending stress with a design problem (the lightest section that meets a deflection limit), to a small finite-element solver that you validate against exact formulas, refine on meshes and use to check a footbridge-style beam, to a moving vehicle whose envelope of shear and moment you build by sweeping, check two independent ways and use to choose a girder. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All models are simplified classroom models (straight elastic beams, small deflections); they are never design tools for real structures. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as a free choice of I-section dimensions, shear deformation and a depth-versus-weight explorer.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
