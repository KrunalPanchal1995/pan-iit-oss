# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** In `lesson.py`, change the load position to 5 m on the 8 m beam. Predict RA and RB first, then run it.
2. **Stretch.** Write `reactions_two_loads(P1, a1, P2, a2, L)` that adds the reactions from two separate point loads, and check that RA + RB = P1 + P2.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, using the moment diagram, why a plank is most likely to break under your feet rather than at the bank.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
