# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openemwave

**Short description:** Learn how antennas make radio waves: charges, fields, wavelength and a wave simulator you can trust, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openemwave is a beginner-friendly learning repo that shows how an antenna radiates. In three short sessions you go from electric charges and field lines, and the idea that light and radio are the same travelling wave, to Coulomb's law, superposition, the wave equation and the length of a half-wave antenna for FM radio and wifi, to your own finite-difference time-domain (FDTD) simulator of electric and magnetic fields that you test against the analytic wave speed, the Courant stability limit, energy conservation, the 1/sqrt(r) fall-off of a cylindrical wave, standing waves at a conductor and the radiation pattern of two sources. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as phased arrays of many elements, better absorbing boundaries, dielectrics and 3D. All models are simplified educational models, not antenna design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
