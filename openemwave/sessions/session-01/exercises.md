# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Change `LIKE` in `lesson.py` so the two charges are +1 and +2. Predict where the field is zero (closer to the weaker charge), then check with `field_at`.
2. **Stretch.** Write `colour_wavelength(f)` for red light (450 THz) and blue light (650 THz) and print both in nanometres. Which is longer?
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why a sound wave of 440 Hz has a wavelength of about 0.78 m, much shorter than the 680 km a radio wave of the same frequency would have. (Hint: compare the two speeds.)

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
