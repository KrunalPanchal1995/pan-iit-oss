# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opencncpath

**Short description:** Learn how a CNC machine follows a toolpath: G-code, offsets, pocketing and a simulation that checks the result, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opencncpath is a beginner-friendly learning repo that teaches how a drawing becomes a CNC toolpath. In five short sessions you go from a cutter that follows a path round a square pocket, to writing and parsing your own G-code with offsets, arcs and a machining-time estimate, to pocketing strategies whose leftover ridges, corners and removed volume you check on a simulated height-map against formulas, and finally to offsetting any convex polygon: where the corners of the cutter path go, when the offset collapses or loses an edge, a hexagonal pocket cut with rings, and finally a pocket with a round boss cut in one unbroken path of lines and arcs, built from the distance to the walls and the boss. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as rest machining, 3D finishing and post-processor dialects. Everything here is educational toolpath geometry: real machining needs trained operators, machine-specific post-processing and safety checks.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
