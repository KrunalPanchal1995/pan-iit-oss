opencncpath

Turn a drawing into a CNC toolpath, in five sessions.

  • Session 1: A cutter follows a path (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: G-code, offsets and arcs (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: Pocketing strategies and a simulation that checks them (Level 3, 90 min)
  • Session 4: Offset any convex polygon (Level 3, 90 min)
  • Session 5: A pocket with a boss, cut without lifting (Level 3, 90 min)
