# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opendcmotor

**Short description:** Learn the DC motor torque-speed curve, gearboxes and PWM speed control, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opendcmotor is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the brushed DC motor, the small motor inside toys, robots, fans and winches. In three short sessions you go from the idea that more load means a slower motor, to the electrical and mechanical equations behind the straight torque-speed line, power and efficiency, and a gear-ratio design problem, to a time-domain simulation (two coupled equations solved with RK4) that you validate against the line and against energy conservation. You then add PWM speed control, a start-up current limit and a stall failure case, and finally size a motor and gearbox for a robot that climbs a slope. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All examples are low-voltage educational models. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as closed-loop speed control, battery sag and thermal limits.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
