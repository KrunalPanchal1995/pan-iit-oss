# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** In `lesson.py`, print the period for m = 4e5 kg and k = 2e7 N/m. Predict it first from the worked example, then check.
2. **Stretch.** Write a small function that returns the ground period at which a building with a given mass and stiffness resonates, and test it on the worked example.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, in two or three sentences and without formulas, why a 20-storey frame is more likely to be hurt by slow, long-period ground shaking than a 3-storey one. Use the rule of thumb.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
