# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Change the power and mass in `lesson.py` to 1500 W and 2 kg. Predict the time to boil, then run it and compare.
2. **Stretch.** Write `energy_to_boil_off(mass)` that returns the energy needed to boil away a given mass at 100 C, and print it for 0.1 kg.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, without any equation, why a surface 200 K above the boiling point can carry about the same heat flux as one 10 K above it.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
