Session 4: boiling curves from fluid properties

Pressure changes the critical heat flux. Pick a pressure and a heat flux, and read the boiling curve, the margin below the CHF and the wall temperature.

This is a simplified educational model. It is not a validated design tool and must not be used for real structures, electrical installations, vehicles, industrial processes or medical decisions. The correlations are textbook ones with large uncertainties (the CHF about 20 to 30 percent, the nucleate flux up to a factor of two), and the transition and film parts, especially at high pressure, show only a shape.
