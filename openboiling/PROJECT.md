# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openboiling

**Short description:** Learn the boiling curve, bubble growth and the burnout (critical heat flux) of a heated surface in 4 sessions.

**Full description:** openboiling is a beginner-friendly learning repo about boiling on a hot surface. In four short sessions you go from why a pot of water stops at 100 C, to the energy and heat-flux numbers that decide how big a kettle element must be, to a time-stepping simulation of a heated plate that shows the boiling curve, the sudden burnout when the critical heat flux is exceeded, the hysteresis of a power-controlled heater and the square-root-of-time growth of a bubble, and in Session 4 to a boiling curve built from the properties of water with Rohsenow, Zuber and Berenson correlations, which shows the critical heat flux rising by a factor of 1.86 from 1 atm to 5 bar. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The models are simplified, use illustrative coefficients (Sessions 1 to 3) or textbook correlations with large uncertainties (Session 4); they are for teaching, not for designing heaters. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
