# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openfatigue

**Short description:** Learn fatigue: S-N curves, damage counting and crack growth, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openfatigue is a beginner-friendly learning repo about fatigue, the way parts fail under repeated loads that are each far below the one-pull strength. In three short sessions you go from a paper clip and a cyclic stress wave, to Basquin's S-N relation, the Goodman mean-stress line, stress concentration and a shaft design, to a full variable-load estimate: rainflow counting, Miner's damage sum, Paris' law crack growth, the large scatter of fatigue life and a bracket whose life and inspection interval you work out from a load history. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All numbers are illustrative and the models are simplified teaching models, never a method for real or safety-critical parts. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
