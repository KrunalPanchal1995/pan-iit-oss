# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opengears

**Short description:** Learn gear ratios, torque, compound trains and a tooth-count search for a gearbox, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opengears is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the gear train, the machine element inside bicycles, drills, winches and car gearboxes. In three short sessions you go from the idea that a big gear driven by a small one turns slowly but strongly (and that an idler only flips the direction), to the mathematics of speed ratio, torque ratio, compound trains, module and centre distance, and a design problem for a 1:20 reduction with whole numbers of teeth, to a Level 3 study that searches every tooth combination with numpy, adds mesh losses, simulates a two-stage train with backlash and a torque pulse and checks it against exact laws, and verifies the planetary (Willis) relation. You then choose a gear train for a hand-crank winch. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All models are simplified educational models, not validated gearbox design tools. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as helical gears, three-stage searches and worm drives.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
