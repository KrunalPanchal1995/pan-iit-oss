# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opengasturbine

**Short description:** Learn how a gas turbine makes power and thrust: the Brayton cycle, the best pressure ratio and a turbojet with real losses, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opengasturbine is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the gas turbine. In three short sessions you go from the four parts of the engine and its energy balance, to the temperatures, the net work and the best pressure ratio of the ideal Brayton cycle, to a turbojet with compressor, turbine and nozzle efficiencies, validated against the ideal cycle and an energy balance, ending in the thrust and fuel flow of a small engine on a test stand. Air is treated as an ideal gas with constant properties, so the numbers are illustrative, not engine data. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as flight speed and ram compression, pressure losses, variable gas properties, turbine cooling and the turbofan.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
