# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openstereodic

**Short description:** Learn how two cameras and a speckle pattern measure 3D shape and strain: parallax, sub-pixel matching and image correlation, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openstereodic is a beginner-friendly learning repo about stereo digital image correlation (stereo DIC), the optical method labs use to measure the shape and strain of a loaded specimen without touching it. In three short sessions you go from a pinhole camera and the parallax rule Z = f B / d, to triangulating matched pixels and matching speckle patches to a fraction of a pixel, to a full 2D correlation engine that you validate against a known strain field, with noise and subset-size experiments. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All images are generated by code, so you always know the exact answer. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it, such as higher-order shape functions, camera calibration and 3D strain maps of curved surfaces.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
