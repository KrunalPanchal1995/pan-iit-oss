# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Run `design_shaft(100)`, `design_shaft(300)` and `design_shaft(900)` and predict first which has the largest margin between d_min and the standard size.
2. **Stretch.** Write `lightest_steel(torque, yields)` that tries a list of yield strengths such as [250, 350, 500] and returns the one with the smallest mass per metre of the standard shaft.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why the safety factor of the standard shaft in Figure 2 jumps up right after each step, and why it never goes below the requested value.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
