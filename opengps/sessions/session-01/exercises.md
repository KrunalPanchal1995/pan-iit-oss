# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Change `me` in `lesson.py` to (80, 50) and predict the two candidates from circles 1 and 2 before you run it.
2. **Stretch.** Write `miss_for_error(e)` that returns the misfit of `locate_2d` when range 3 has an error of e km. Plot it for e from -5 to 5.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, with a sketch, why a third beacon placed on the line through the first two cannot choose between the two candidates.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
