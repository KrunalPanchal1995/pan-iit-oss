# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openhydraulics

**Short description:** Learn Pascal's law and hydraulic machines: pressure, force multiplication and a simulated garage lift, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openhydraulics is a beginner-friendly learning repo that explains how a hydraulic jack lifts a car with a small force. In three short sessions you go from the idea that pressure in a liquid acts equally everywhere, to the equations of pressure, Pascal's law, the work balance and hydrostatic pressure with a real design problem (piston diameters, lever and pumps per centimetre for a 1200 kg car), to a time-stepping simulation of a pump, cylinder, relief valve, leakage, friction and slightly compressible oil that you validate against the exact lift speed v = Q / A and an energy balance. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All models are simplified educational models, not design tools. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as check valves, air in the oil, cylinder walls and controlled lowering.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
