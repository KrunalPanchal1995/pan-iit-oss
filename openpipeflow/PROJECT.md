# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openpipeflow

**Short description:** Learn pipe flow and pressure loss: choose a pipe size that meets the flow within a pump budget.

**Full description:** openpipeflow is a beginner-friendly learning repo about water in pipes. In three short sessions you go from continuity and Bernoulli (why a narrowing pipe speeds the water up and lowers its pressure), to the Reynolds number, the Darcy-Weisbach friction loss and the pump power you must pay for, and finally to a small looped town network solved with the Hardy Cross method and validated against the single-pipe formula, mass conservation and an analytic laminar result. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as general networks, pumps and tanks and a daily demand cycle. It is an educational model, not a hydraulic design tool.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
