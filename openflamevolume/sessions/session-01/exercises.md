# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** In `lesson.py` change `B` to 0.05 m and 0.20 m and predict, then check, how the Monte Carlo spread at Z = 2 m changes.
2. **Stretch.** Write `depth_from_two_rays` that triangulates by intersecting the two rays (left ray from the origin, right ray from (B, 0, 0)) instead of using the disparity formula, and check it agrees with `triangulate`.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why moving the cameras twice as far from the flame makes the depth error four times larger, and what you would change to get the old error back.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
