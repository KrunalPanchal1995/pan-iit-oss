openflamevolume

Rebuild a 3D flame from many 2D photos using stereo image correlation, in three sessions.

  • Session 1: Two eyes see depth (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Find the same spot in two photos (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: From many photos to one 3D flame (Level 3, 90 min)
