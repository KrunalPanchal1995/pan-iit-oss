# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opencalculus

**Short description:** Slopes and areas on a moving car's graphs: derivatives, integrals and how to compute them numerically, in 5 sessions.

**Full description:** opencalculus is a beginner-friendly learning repo that makes calculus visible. In five short sessions you go from the slope of a distance-time graph being speed and the area under a speed-time graph being distance, to the derivative as a limit of slopes, the integral as a sum of thin rectangles, the fundamental theorem and a braking-distance design problem, and finally to numerical differentiation and integration whose errors you compare with exact answers (orders 1, 2, 2 and 4, the round-off limit, adaptive steps and failure cases) and to Romberg integration, which reaches an error below 1e-12 for the area of e^x with 17 function values, and to Gauss-Legendre quadrature, which reaches 6.5e-13 with only 5. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as derivatives without subtraction, noise-robust derivatives and ODE solvers.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
