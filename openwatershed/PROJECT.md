# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openwatershed

**Short description:** Learn how rainfall becomes a river flood peak: run-off, unit hydrograph, urbanisation and a detention pond, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openwatershed is a beginner-friendly learning repo about flood hydrology. In three short sessions you go from the idea that some rain soaks in and the rest runs off into the river, to the rational method and the SCS curve number with a made-up design problem, to a unit hydrograph built by convolution, Muskingum channel routing, the effect of urbanisation and the sizing of a detention pond. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All models are simplified teaching models: real flood forecasts and designs come from official agencies and qualified hydrologists. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
