# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openepidemic

**Short description:** Learn the SIR outbreak model: R0, herd immunity, fitting noisy data and testing vaccination (teaching tool, synthetic data).

**Full description:** openepidemic is a beginner-friendly learning repo about how outbreaks are modelled. In three short sessions you go from susceptible, infected and recovered people as three boxes with arrows, to the SIR equations with R0, herd immunity and the final size, to a full simulation with your own Runge-Kutta solver, a least-squares fit of beta and gamma to synthetic noisy case counts, a stochastic version in which small outbreaks die out by chance, and a test of vaccination. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. IMPORTANT: this is general education. All data are synthetic. The models are teaching tools: they are not forecasts, not medical advice and not a basis for public-health decisions. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as age groups, an exposed stage and better uncertainty.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
