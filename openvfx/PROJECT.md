# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openvfx

**Short description:** Learn how film and game characters bend: matrices, bones and skinning, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openvfx is a beginner-friendly learning repo that teaches how digital characters are moved in film and games. In three short sessions you go from a single rotation matrix, to chained bones that rotate about joints, to linear blend skinning whose defects you measure and check against a hand calculation. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as helper bones, volume-keeping skinning and 3D characters.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
