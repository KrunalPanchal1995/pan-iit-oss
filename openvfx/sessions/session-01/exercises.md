# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Change the angles in `lesson.py` to 45 and 135 degrees. Predict where the hand lands before you run it.
2. **Stretch.** Write `rotate_triangle(theta)` that turns the triangle (0,0), (1,0), (0,1) by 60 degrees and check that all three side lengths are unchanged.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why rotating by 30 degrees and then by 60 degrees is the same as rotating by 90 degrees. (Hint: multiply the two matrices.)

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
