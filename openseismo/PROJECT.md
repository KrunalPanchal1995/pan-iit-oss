# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openseismo

**Short description:** Learn how seismologists locate an earthquake: S-P times, circles, least squares and error ellipses, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openseismo is a beginner-friendly learning repo about locating an earthquake from the arrival times of its waves. In three short sessions you go from the gap between P and S waves (the lightning-and-thunder rule), to the maths of distance and three-circle trilateration, to the real-world version in which the origin time is unknown and the arrival picks are noisy: Geiger's least-squares method, Monte Carlo pick errors, station geometry, a layered-Earth failure case, a simple magnitude formula and a 95 percent error ellipse. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All data are synthetic. The repo is educational only: it is not an earthquake early-warning or prediction tool, and earthquakes cannot be predicted. It is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
