# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openprojectile

**Short description:** Learn projectile motion and the effect of air drag on a cricket ball: equations, RK4 simulation and a six-hitting design problem, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openprojectile is a beginner-friendly learning repo about how a thrown or hit ball flies. Session 1 shows that sideways and up-and-down motion are independent and lets you explore range, peak height and flight time. Session 2 turns that into equations: the range formula, the 45 degree rule, the two angles for one range, the effect of launch height, and a design problem (the lowest speed that hits a target). Session 3 adds quadratic air drag for a cricket ball (approximate typical values), solves the motion by RK4 in pure numpy, validates the solver against the exact no-drag answers and against an energy balance, finds numerically how drag lowers the best angle below 45 degrees, studies terminal speed, drag coefficient and wind, and shows a failure case with explicit Euler. Every session has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo stops on purpose: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
