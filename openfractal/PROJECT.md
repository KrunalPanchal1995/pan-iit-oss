# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openfractal

**Short description:** Learn complex numbers, the escape-time algorithm, Mandelbrot and Julia sets from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openfractal is a beginner-friendly learning repo that teaches how fractal pictures and zoom videos are made. In three short sessions you go from complex numbers as points that slide and turn, to the escape-time algorithm and the Mandelbrot and Julia sets, to a fast numpy renderer with smooth colouring that you check against known points, against a Monte Carlo estimate of the set's area, and against the limits of floating-point numbers. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as zooms far deeper than double precision allows, faster interior tests and a looping video.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
