# Coding tasks: Session 2

1. **Easy.** Print the capacity of the mug for R = 4.5 cm, t = 0.5 cm, H = 9 cm and compare with the app, then find the radius that gives 250 mL.
2. **Stretch.** Add a lip: make a thicker rim by subtracting a smaller cavity near the top (a second box) in `body2d` and plot it.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, with the formulas, why the smooth union with k = 2 cm pulls the surface out by 0.5 cm at the crotch of the handle.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
