# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openincline

**Short description:** Learn why a block on a ramp holds or slides: forces, friction and the slide angle, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openincline is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the most useful picture in mechanics: a block on a tilted ramp. In three short sessions you go from a free-body diagram where friction is a limit, to the equations of static and kinetic friction with a ramp design problem, to a time-stepping simulation that switches from stuck to sliding and is validated against the exact slide angle atan(mu) and the exact acceleration. You also test how uncertainty in the friction coefficient changes the answer, compare with a rolling cylinder, and finish with a real mini-project: measure the friction coefficient with a board and a phone angle app. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
