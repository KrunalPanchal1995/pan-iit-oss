# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Change `mu` in `lesson.py` to 0.3 and 0.9 and predict the critical angle before you run it (use arctan).
2. **Stretch.** Write `forces_table(m, mu)` that prints N, the pull, the limit and the net force for angles 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 degrees.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, in two sentences and without a formula, why a heavy and a light block start to slide at the same angle.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
