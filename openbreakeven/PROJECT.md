# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openbreakeven

**Short description:** Learn break-even analysis: fixed and variable cost, margin of safety, risk and payback, from scratch in 5 sessions.

**Full description:** openbreakeven is a beginner-friendly learning repo about one question every small business asks: how much must we sell before we stop losing money? In five short sessions you go from a made-up lemonade stall, to the cost-volume-profit equations of a made-up bakery (contribution margin, break-even, margin of safety, target profit, operating leverage), to a richer model with several products, a second oven, random prices and demand, a ranking of which input matters most, and payback with discounting, and in Session 4 to a model in which price and demand are correlated, checked against exact formulas, that shows how the link changes the chance of a loss month, and in Session 5 to a year with seasons and a loan repaid in monthly instalments, which shows how much extra cash a seasonal business needs. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All businesses and numbers are made up and the repo is general education, not financial advice. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
