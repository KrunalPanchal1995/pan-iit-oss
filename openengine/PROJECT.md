# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openengine

**Short description:** Learn how engines work: heat, work, P-V loops, Otto and Diesel efficiency, from scratch in 5 sessions.

**Full description:** openengine is a beginner-friendly learning repo about heat engines. In three short sessions you go from the energy balance of an engine (heat in, work out, heat rejected) and the idea that a P-V loop's area is the work, to the ideal Otto and Diesel cycles with their efficiency formulas and the Carnot limit, to a step-by-step numerical simulation with a Wiebe burn, heat loss and friction that you validate against the formulas and the first law. You also compare Otto and Diesel fairly, meet the knock limit, and choose a compression ratio for a small generator engine. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models, not engine design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
