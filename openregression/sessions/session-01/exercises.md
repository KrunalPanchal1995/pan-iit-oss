# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Change the guess line in `lesson.py` to 0.5 + 0.40 x and print its total squared miss. Is it better than 1.0 + 0.30 x?
2. **Stretch.** Write `forecast(rain_mm)` that uses the best line and prints the yield for 400, 600 and 800 mm.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, in two sentences, why the line that minimises the sum of the absolute residuals could be a different line from the least-squares line.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
