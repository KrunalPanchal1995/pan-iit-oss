# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opentruss

**Short description:** Learn how truss bridges carry load: statics, the method of joints and a stiffness solver, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opentruss is a beginner-friendly learning repo about truss bridges. In three short sessions you go from why a triangle is rigid and a rectangle is not, to the method of joints and the method of sections on a Warren truss, to a general direct-stiffness solver written in numpy that finds every bar force and joint deflection, which you check against hand calculations, equilibrium at every joint and a virtual-work formula. You compare Pratt, Howe and Warren shapes under the same load, see what a missing bar does to the matrix, and pick the lightest truss for a 12 m footbridge in an illustrative mini-project. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The models are educational and simplified and are never for designing real structures. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as moving loads, buckling-aware sizing and 3D trusses.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
