Session 5: where is the axis?

A noisy candle photo, 79 pixel columns and 20 rows, whose axis is not in the middle. Two finders look for the axis in the photo alone; the dashed line, which they never see, is the truth.

This is a simplified educational model. It is not a validated design tool and must not be used for real structures, electrical installations, vehicles, industrial processes or medical decisions.
