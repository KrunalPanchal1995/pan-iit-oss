# Coding tasks: Session 3

1. **Easy.** Use `smooth_invert` on a noisy photo of a Gaussian with lam = 0.01, 0.3 and 10 and describe, in two sentences each, what you see.
2. **Stretch.** Change the number of rings in `noise_gain` from 40 to 20 and 80 and describe how the gain at the axis ring changes; explain it with the pixel size.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why averaging the left and right halves of a real photo reduces the noise by about a factor sqrt(2), and why this is only allowed if the axis position is known.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
