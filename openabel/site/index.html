openabel

Recover a 3D flame slice from one 2D photo, in five sessions.

  • Session 1: A photo adds up the light along every line of sight (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: From a radial recipe to a photo: the forward Abel transform (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: Back from the photo: onion peeling, noise and smoothing (Level 3, 90 min)
  • Session 4: No peeking at the truth: choosing the smoothing from the photo alone (Level 3, 90 min)
  • Session 5: Where is the axis? Finding the flame's symmetry axis from the photo (Level 3, 90 min)
