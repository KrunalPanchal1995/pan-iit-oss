# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opensurvey

**Short description:** Learn surveying: levelling, total-station coordinates and traverse adjustment, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opensurvey is a beginner-friendly learning repo about how land is measured before anything is built on it. In three short sessions you go from the height difference given by a level and a staff, to a levelling book checked by arithmetic, slope distances and total-station coordinates, and then to a closed traverse that is adjusted with the Bowditch rule, whose area is found by the coordinate method and whose errors are propagated by a seeded Monte Carlo simulation. You also meet curvature and refraction on long sights, and a blunder that the adjustment hides and how to find it. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All data are synthetic and the models are simplified: this is education, not a survey you may rely on for legal boundaries or construction setting-out. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as least-squares adjustment, levelling networks and earthwork volumes.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
