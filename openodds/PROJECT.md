# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openodds

**Short description:** Learn probability by counting and by Monte Carlo simulation: dice, cards, birthdays and lotteries, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openodds is a beginner-friendly learning repo about chance. In three short sessions you go from listing the equally likely outcomes of two dice, to counting rules (permutations, combinations, 5-card poker hands, the birthday problem and expected value), to Monte Carlo simulation with a seeded generator, its standard error, and the failure case of rare events such as a 1 in 13,983,816 lottery. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. This is education about chance, not betting advice. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as importance sampling, full poker tables and conditional probability.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
