# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Print the exact probability of every sum of THREE dice using `sum_probabilities(3)` and name the most likely sums. Predict them first.
2. **Stretch.** Write `prob_at_least(target)` that adds the exact probabilities of all sums from `target` to 12, and check that `prob_at_least(2)` is 1.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why the simulated bars never match the exact bars perfectly, even with a million rolls, and what you would see if you changed the seed.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
