# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openfridge

**Short description:** Learn how a refrigerator moves heat from cold to warm: energy balance, COP, the Carnot limit and a toy vapour-compression cycle, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openfridge is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the refrigeration cycle. In three short sessions you go from the four parts of a fridge and the idea that heat only moves from cold to warm when work is supplied, to the coefficient of performance (COP), the Carnot limit in kelvin and the sizing of a compressor for a heat leak, to a simplified vapour-compression cycle on a pressure-enthalpy diagram with a compressor efficiency, validated against the energy balance and the Carnot bound, ending in a yearly energy estimate that includes room temperature and door openings. The working fluid is a toy refrigerant with illustrative numbers, not a real one. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as real refrigerant data, subcooling, heat-exchanger sizing and thermostat cycling.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
