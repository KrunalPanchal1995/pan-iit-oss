# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openheatspread

**Short description:** Learn heat conduction and predict laptop hot spots: Fourier's law, thermal resistance and a 2D finite-difference solver, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openheatspread is a beginner-friendly learning repo that teaches how heat moves through solids and how engineers keep electronics cool. In three short sessions you go from the temperature profile through a single slab, to thermal resistance networks that decide whether a chip stays under its temperature limit, to a small 2D heat solver that you validate against exact solutions before using it on a laptop board with two hot spots. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as implicit solvers, heat pipes and sensor-based hot-spot detection. All models are simplified educational models, not thermal design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
