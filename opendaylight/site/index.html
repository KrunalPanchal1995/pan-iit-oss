opendaylight

Plan windows for natural daylight using the sun path, in three sessions.

  • Session 1: Where is the sun? Sun path, shadows and day length (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Sunlight on a window, the overhang and solar heat gain (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: Simulate a year of daylight and heat, then plan the window (Level 3, 90 min)
