# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opendaylight

**Short description:** Learn to plan windows for natural daylight: sun path, shadows, overhangs and energy, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opendaylight is a beginner-friendly learning repo about daylight and windows in buildings. In three short sessions you go from where the sun is in the sky (declination, noon altitude, the sun-path diagram, shadows, day length and Zero Shadow Days in Indian cities), to how sunlight meets a window (angle of incidence, the vertical shadow angle, sizing an overhang or chajja, clear-sky sunlight and solar heat gain through glass), to an hour-by-hour simulation of a study room for a whole year that adds the daylight factor, dimmed lights and air-conditioning energy. You validate it against an independent 3D ray test, hand calculations and limits, and use it to choose the window orientation, size and overhang for a room in Chennai. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target window planner and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models, not building design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
