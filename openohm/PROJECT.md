# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openohm

**Short description:** Learn V = I R and P = V I, design resistor circuits to a current and power limit, and solve an LED circuit numerically.

**Full description:** openohm is a beginner-friendly learning repo on the first circuit law every engineer uses. In three short sessions you go from Ohm's law and electrical power, to series and parallel resistor networks with power ratings and a design problem, to a non-ohmic LED solved by Newton's method and validated against bisection, the ohmic limit and a hand calculation. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All circuits are low-voltage educational models (never mains). The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as automatic network synthesis, self-heating resistors and tolerance analysis.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
