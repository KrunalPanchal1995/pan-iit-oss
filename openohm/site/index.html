openohm

Ohm's law to a safe resistor design, in three sessions.

  • Session 1: Ohm's law and electrical power (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Resistor networks, power ratings and a design to a limit (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: A non-ohmic LED: solve it numerically and validate (Level 3, 90 min)
