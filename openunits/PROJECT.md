# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openunits

**Short description:** Learn units, dimensions and error growth, then audit a calculation like the one behind a 1999 Mars mission loss.

**Full description:** openunits is a beginner-friendly learning repo about one of the most practical skills in science and engineering: keeping track of units. In three short sessions you build a unit converter and learn why a number without a unit is not an answer, treat dimensions as exponent vectors of mass, length and time to check equations and to see how a small unit mistake grows, and finally simulate a toy spacecraft trajectory correction in which thruster impulse in pound-force seconds is read as newton seconds (a factor of 4.448222). You also write a tiny units-checked wrapper in numpy only, with no external unit library. The loss of NASA's Mars Climate Orbiter in 1999 is the motivating story; the spacecraft numbers in the toy model are illustrative, not mission data. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
