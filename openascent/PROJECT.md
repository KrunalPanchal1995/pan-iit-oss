# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openascent

**Short description:** Fly a rocket to orbit: the rocket equation, staging, gravity and drag losses and a gravity-turn ascent, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openascent is a beginner-friendly learning repo about how rockets reach orbit. In three short sessions you go from the rocket equation, the speed of a circular orbit and the reason rockets drop empty stages, to a vertical flight stepped in time with Euler and Runge-Kutta 4 that shows the gravity and drag losses and why going straight up never gives an orbit, to a two-stage launch around a round Earth with a pitch kick, a gravity turn, staging and an upper-stage burn into a 200 km orbit. You validate the simulation by closing the delta-v budget, recovering the rocket equation and checking energy conservation, find the narrow pitch-kick window, and work out the largest payload the launcher can carry. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target flight and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models, not launch vehicle design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
