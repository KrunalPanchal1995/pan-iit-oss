openchaos

See chaos in a double pendulum, in five sessions.

  • Session 1: Two almost identical starts drift apart (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Equations, energy and a trustworthy simulation (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: Measuring chaos and the limits of forecasting (Level 3, 90 min)
  • Session 4: A fourth-order symplectic solver (Level 3, 90 min)
  • Session 5: Ensemble forecasts: probabilities instead of paths (Level 3, 90 min)
