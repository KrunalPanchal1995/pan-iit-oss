# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openchaos

**Short description:** Learn why a double pendulum is unpredictable: equations, energy checks, Lyapunov exponents and a flip-time map, in 5 sessions.

**Full description:** openchaos is a beginner-friendly learning repo about deterministic chaos. In five short sessions you go from a single pendulum, which is predictable, to a double pendulum where two starts a hundred-millionth of a radian apart end up in different places; then to the equations of motion, with energy conservation and the small-angle normal modes used to check a simulation; and then to a Runge-Kutta 4 solver compared with a symplectic one, a measured Lyapunov exponent, a flip-time map of regular and chaotic starts, the predictability horizon of a forecast, and finally a fourth-order symplectic solver built from the midpoint rule, compared by the work it costs, and then ensemble forecasts that turn a cloud of 1000 runs into a flip probability with its sampling and step errors. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as the full Lyapunov spectrum, damped real pendulums and chaos for unequal masses.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
