# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openheatpump

**Short description:** Learn why a heat pump beats an electric heater: COP, Carnot limit, heat loss, sizing and an hourly winter simulation.

**Full description:** openheatpump is a beginner-friendly learning repo about heating a home. In three short sessions you go from the big idea (a heater makes heat, a heat pump moves it, so one kilowatt-hour of electricity can give several kilowatt-hours of heat), to the mathematics (heating COP, the Carnot limit in kelvin, how COP falls as the weather gets colder, the heat loss of a house and how to size the machine), to a real simulation: an hourly winter with a house that stores heat, a thermostat, a COP that depends on outdoor and supply temperature, a defrost penalty and a backup heater. You check the simulation against energy conservation and a hand calculation, test how insulation, supply temperature and tariff change the bill, and find out what happens on the coldest night when the heat pump is too small. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where to take it next. It is educational only and not an installation design.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
