# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openevacuation

**Short description:** Plan a safe building evacuation with crowd flow: doors, density, stairs, queues and RSET, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openevacuation is a beginner-friendly learning repo about how people leave a building in an emergency. In three short sessions you go from a crowd passing a door like water through a pipe (flow = density x speed x width, effective width, and evacuation time = pre-movement + walking + queueing), to the speed-density law of real crowds, its maximum flow and why pushing makes things slower, and conservation of flow that tells you where queues form, to a step-by-step network simulation of a multi-storey block that you validate against hand formulas and conservation of people. You then compare the required safe escape time (RSET) with the available safe escape time (ASET), check the case of a stair blocked by smoke, and plan the stairs of a student hostel. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models, not fire-safety design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
