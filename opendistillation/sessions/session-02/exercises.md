1. **Easy.** Use `bubble_point` to print T and y for x = 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.40 and 0.80 and check them against the T-x-y figure.

3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, using the activity coefficients, why the wash boils at 86.6 C and not at the 96.8 C that Raoult's law predicts.
