# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opendistillation

**Short description:** Learn distillation: separate ethanol and water with a column of stages, from boiling points to McCabe-Thiele design, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opendistillation is a beginner-friendly learning repo about distillation, the most used separation process in the chemical industry. In three short sessions you go from one boil-and-condense stage, the relative volatility and the material balance of a column, with the Fenske equation for the minimum number of stages, to the real ethanol-water equilibrium built from Antoine vapour pressures and van Laar activity coefficients, with T-x-y and x-y diagrams and the azeotrope that stops ordinary distillation near 95.6 percent by mass, to a McCabe-Thiele design of a distillery column with operating lines, the feed stage, minimum reflux (including a tangent pinch), reboiler duty and the reflux ratio that minimises cost. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target dynamic column and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models, not plant design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
