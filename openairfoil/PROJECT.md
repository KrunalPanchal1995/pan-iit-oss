# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openairfoil

**Short description:** Learn how wings work: lift, drag, airfoil shape and a panel method, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openairfoil is a beginner-friendly learning repo about wings and airfoils. The shape of a wing decides its lift and drag, and therefore a plane's fuel use. In three short sessions you go from dynamic pressure, the lift equation, the stall speed and Bernoulli's pressure coefficient, to NACA 4-digit airfoils, thin-airfoil theory and the drag polar of a whole aircraft, to a source-and-vortex panel method that computes the pressure all round any airfoil. You validate the panel method against the exact flow past a circle and two independent lift calculations, add a skin-friction drag estimate, and choose a wing section for a small two-seater. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target and a roadmap (boundary layer, drag polar, stall, lifting line) show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models, not aircraft design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
