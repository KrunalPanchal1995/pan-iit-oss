openairfoil

Estimate airfoil lift and drag with a panel method, in three sessions.

  • Session 1: Lift, drag and the pressure around a wing (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Airfoil shape, thin-airfoil theory and the drag polar (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: A panel method for lift, and an estimate of drag (Level 3, 90 min)
