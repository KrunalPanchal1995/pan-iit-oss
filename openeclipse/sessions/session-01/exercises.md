# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Print the angular diameter of the Sun and the Moon with `sky_radii()` and explain in one sentence why the two numbers are so close.
2. **Stretch.** Write `eclipse_kind(sep, moon_distance)` that returns total, annular, partial or none, and test it on the Moon at 360000 km and 400000 km.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why a 200 km wide shadow moving at 0.558 km/s over the ground gives about 6 minutes of darkness at most, and why real totality is shorter than the Moon-Earth geometry might suggest.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
