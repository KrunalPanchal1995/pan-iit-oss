# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openloan

**Short description:** Learn how loan interest compounds and how to compare EMIs, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openloan is a beginner-friendly learning repo about borrowing money. In three short sessions you go from the idea that you repay more than you borrowed, to compound growth and the EMI formula checked by a month-by-month simulation, to a small loan simulator that handles prepayment, a changing rate, flat versus reducing-balance offers, inflation and loans that are too big. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All lenders, rates and amounts are made up and the repo is general education, not financial advice. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
