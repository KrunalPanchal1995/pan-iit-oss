# Coding tasks: Session 3

1. **Easy.** Solve the 3-4-5 triangle from the guess (0, 0), (3, 0.5), (0.5, 2), then from (0, 0), (3, 0.5), (0.5, -2), and compare the two answers.
2. **Stretch.** Add the statement ('perp', 1, 0, 2) to the L-bracket sketch at the right corner, where the lines meet at p1, and check the report says 'redundant'.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why a sketch that has two degrees of freedom left can still be solved, and what the solver does with the freedom.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
