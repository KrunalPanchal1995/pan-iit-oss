# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Change the speed in `lesson.py` to 1.0 and then to 0.2, and predict the stripe slope before you run it.
2. **Stretch.** Make a kymograph from a row 3 pixels above the centre of the same video and show that the stripe is dimmer but has the same slope.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why a kymograph of a blob that crosses the line at an angle gives a different pixel speed than the real speed of the blob.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
