# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opendesalination

**Short description:** Estimate the energy to turn seawater into drinking water: osmotic pressure, least work and a reverse osmosis simulation in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opendesalination is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the energy of seawater desalination. In three short sessions you go from the osmotic pressure of seawater (the van 't Hoff law) and the idea that pressure times volume is energy, to the least work of separation as the area under a rising pressure curve, the role of recovery, brine, stages and energy recovery devices, to a step-by-step simulation of a reverse osmosis membrane vessel that you validate against a closed-form answer, the salt balance and the second law. You also choose the feed pressure of a seawater vessel that meets recovery and drinking-water limits. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target energy ladder and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models, not plant design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
