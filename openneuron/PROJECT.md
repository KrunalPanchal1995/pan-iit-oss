# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openneuron

**Short description:** Learn how a nerve cell fires and how its signal travels: threshold, RC membrane, and Hodgkin-Huxley, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openneuron is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the electrical signalling of nerve cells (general education only, not medical information). In three short sessions you go from an all-or-nothing spike in a threshold model, to the membrane as an RC circuit (the leaky integrate-and-fire neuron, its firing-rate formula derived and checked numerically), to the Hodgkin-Huxley model of the squid giant axon integrated with your own Runge-Kutta code. You measure the spike threshold, the refractory period, the current at which repetitive firing starts and the maximum firing rate, test the model against the resting state, a charge balance and the leaky-integrator closed form, explore temperature, sodium conductance and axon diameter, and see why a too-large time step blows the simulation up. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
