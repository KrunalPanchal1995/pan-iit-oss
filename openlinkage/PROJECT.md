# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openlinkage

**Short description:** Learn linkages: four-bar position, Grashof's rule, transmission angle and a tuned walking leg, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openlinkage is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the mathematics of mechanisms. In three short sessions you go from rigid bars joined by pins, where turning one crank moves everything else, to the four-bar linkage as equations (Grashof's rule, the position problem solved two ways, the transmission angle and a design problem), and finally to a simulated walking leg: a point on the coupler traces a curve whose stance part must be almost straight. You validate the simulation (loop-closure error, constant bar lengths, velocity by finite differences and by the analytic Jacobian, speed ratio against statics), measure stride, clearance and straightness, test a 1 percent error in one bar, meet a failure case where the crank locks at a dead centre, and tune a leg in a mini-project. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation of a multi-legged walker and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
