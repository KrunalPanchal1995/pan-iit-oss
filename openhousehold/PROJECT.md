# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openhousehold

**Short description:** Model a household's daily electricity use, bill and rooftop solar in 3 short sessions.

**Full description:** openhousehold is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the electricity use of an ordinary home. In three short sessions you go from the idea that energy is power times time, to a 24-hour load profile with a slab tariff, peak demand and power factor, to a month-long simulation with random usage, a rooftop solar array and net metering, checked against hand calculations. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The tariff is an illustrative teaching tariff, not any real utility's. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as batteries, time-of-use tariffs and fitting the model to a real meter.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
