# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** In `lesson.py` change the heater to 2 h at 3000 W. Predict the new total before you run it, then check.
2. **Stretch.** Add a new appliance to `APPLIANCES` (for example a 1000 W iron for 0.5 h) and print its share of the day's total energy as a percentage.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why a 40 W fan on all night (8 h) uses 0.32 kWh while a 2 kW iron used for 10 minutes uses about 0.33 kWh. Which is the bigger load at one moment, and which is the bigger energy user?

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
