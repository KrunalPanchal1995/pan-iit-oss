# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openinduction

**Short description:** Learn how generators and transformers work from Faraday's law: flux, EMF, AC, RMS, turns ratio, losses, saturation.

**Full description:** openinduction is a beginner-friendly learning repo about electromagnetic induction. In three short sessions you go from a magnet pushed through a coil, to Faraday's law with units (EMF = -N dPhi/dt, the sine wave of a rotating coil, RMS values, the 50 Hz grid and the ideal transformer), to a time-domain simulation: a generator with armature resistance and inductance solved with the Runge-Kutta method and checked against the exact steady state, a transformer with copper and core losses, an energy-conservation check that shows Lenz's law at work, and a core that saturates and distorts the output. A mini-project asks you to size a bicycle dynamo for a 3 W lamp. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All models are simplified, low-voltage teaching models, not mains experiments and not design tools. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as three-phase power, rectifiers, coupled-coil transformers and hysteresis.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
