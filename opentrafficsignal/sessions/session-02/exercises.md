1. **Easy.** Print the uniform delay for g = 20, 27, 34 s with C = 60 s and q = 0.15 cars per second, and say how the delay changes with g.

3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, with a sketch of the cumulative curves, why the area between them divided by the number of cars is the average delay.
