1. **Easy.** Use `design_pack(9.0, 4.0, 1.10, 1.0)` and check by hand the number of cells and the zinc mass it prints.

3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why a 12 V, 2 Ah pack of these cells weighs much more than its zinc alone and why `metal_g` is only a minimum.
