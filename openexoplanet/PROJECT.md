# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openexoplanet

**Short description:** Find exoplanets from dips in starlight: transit depth, duration, period search and false alarms, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openexoplanet is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the transit method, the way most known exoplanets were found. In three short sessions you go from the idea that a planet in front of its star blocks a little light, to the mathematics of transit depth, limb darkening, duration and the noise needed to see an Earth, to a full search of a noisy synthetic light curve with detrending, Box Least Squares, an odd-even false-positive check and a measured false-alarm rate. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All data are synthetic and seeded. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
