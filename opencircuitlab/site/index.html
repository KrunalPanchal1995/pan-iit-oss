opencircuitlab

Build series and parallel circuits and solve them with Kirchhoff's laws.

  • Session 1: A battery, a switch and bulbs: series and parallel (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Kirchhoff's laws, dividers and the loading effect (Level 2, 80 min)
  • Session 3: Nodal analysis by matrix, Thevenin and the sensor bridge (Level 3, 90 min)
  • Session 4: Read a netlist: text in, voltages out (Level 3, 90 min)
  • Session 5: A nonlinear part: the diode and Newton's method (Level 3, 90 min)
