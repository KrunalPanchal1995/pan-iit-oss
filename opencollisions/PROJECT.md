# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opencollisions

**Short description:** Learn how bodies move after a collision: momentum, restitution, energy lost and off-centre hits of discs, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opencollisions is a beginner-friendly learning repo about collisions. In three short sessions you go from momentum conservation in sticking and bouncing collisions on a line, to the coefficient of restitution, the energy lost and the ballistic pendulum, to collisions of two smooth discs in two dimensions with an impact parameter, validated against a time-stepping spring contact simulation, ending in a work-energy estimate for a car stopping against a wall. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as friction and spin, many bodies, rotation, crumple-zone force curves and a bouncing ball in a box.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
