# Coding tasks: Session 2

1. **Easy.** Use `collide` to find the speeds for e = 0, 0.5 and 1 when m1 = m2 = 1 kg, v1 = 3 m/s and v2 = 0. Predict first what the equal masses do.

3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why the energy lost depends on the relative speed and not on the speed of each body.
