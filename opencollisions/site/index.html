opencollisions

Predict what happens in collisions with momentum and energy, in three sessions.

  • Session 1: Momentum is kept, energy may not be: sticking and bouncing (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Every collision in between: restitution, energy lost and the ballistic pendulum (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: Off-centre hits: discs in two dimensions, a contact simulation and a crash estimate (Level 3, 90 min)
