# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openphasediagram

**Short description:** Learn phase diagrams: liquidus, solidus, the lever rule, eutectics and fast cooling, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openphasediagram is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the map every metallurgist reads: the phase diagram. In three short sessions you go from the idea that a pure metal melts at one temperature while an alloy melts over a range, to the lever rule that turns a tie line into phase fractions and compositions, to a eutectic alloy whose cooling curve you simulate with latent heat. You then compare equilibrium freezing with fast Scheil-type freezing, see why fast-cooled alloys are not uniform (coring), and find where the equilibrium assumption fails. All diagrams are clearly labelled illustrations with straight-line boundaries, not measured design data. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as homogenising anneals, peritectic diagrams and moving freezing fronts.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
