# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openheartflow

**Short description:** Learn how the heart as a pump and the vessels as pipes create blood pressure, in 3 sessions of Python.

**Full description:** openheartflow is a beginner-friendly learning repo that models blood circulation as a pump pushing blood through pipes. In three short sessions you go from a closed loop where flow equals pressure difference over resistance, to Poiseuille's law (half the radius means 16 times the resistance), cardiac output and mean arterial pressure, to the two-element Windkessel model in which elastic arteries store blood during the heartbeat. You solve it with your own RK4 code, check it against an exact decay and a mean-flow balance, stiffen the arteries, and break the model on purpose. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. This is general education only: it is not medical advice and the model is not a diagnostic tool. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as a heart valve, a pressure-dependent compliance and an arterial network.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
