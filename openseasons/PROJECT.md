# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openseasons

**Short description:** Day, night, seasons and Moon phases explained with geometry, simulated in 3 short sessions.

**Full description:** openseasons is a beginner-friendly learning repo that explains why the Earth has day and night, why seasons come from the tilt of the axis and not from the distance to the Sun, and why the Moon shows phases. In three short sessions you go from simple pictures and dot products, to 3-D vectors for the tilted axis (declination, day length, noon Sun height, the inverse-square law, the sidereal day), to a simulation of the Moon: the synodic month, a phase calendar for 2026 compared with the almanac, moonrise 50 minutes later each day, the odds of an eclipse, and a year table for your own latitude. Each session has the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
