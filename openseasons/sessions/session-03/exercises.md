# Coding tasks: Session 3

1. **Easy.** Print the dates of the first quarter Moons of 2026 from `phase_calendar` and find the one nearest your birthday.
2. **Stretch.** Change the reference epoch by 1 day and see how the full Moon dates move; explain what an error of 1 day in the epoch means.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why a half-lit Moon happens slightly before the exact quarter, using the 389 times larger distance of the Sun.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
