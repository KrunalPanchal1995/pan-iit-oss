opencomfort

Cool a building passively with shading and ventilation, in three sessions.

  • Session 1: Where the heat in a hot room comes from (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Size a sunshade and a breeze (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: Simulate a hot day and choose a passive design (Level 3, 90 min)
