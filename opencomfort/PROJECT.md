# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opencomfort

**Short description:** Cool a building passively: heat gains, sunshades, cross ventilation and a day-long room simulation, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opencomfort is a beginner-friendly learning repo about passive cooling of buildings. In three short sessions you go from the heat balance of a hot room (conduction through walls and roof, sunshine through glass, people and lamps, and air flow) and its steady indoor temperature, to the sun's position in the sky, the shadow of a sunshade (chajja) over a window and the air flow from wind and warm air through open windows, to an hour-by-hour simulation of a top-floor room in Chennai with a sunlit roof and thermal mass that you validate against the steady formula and the energy balance. You compare sunshades, a white roof, all-day ventilation and night flushing against an adaptive comfort limit, and choose the cheapest passive package for a real room. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target figure and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models, not building design tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
