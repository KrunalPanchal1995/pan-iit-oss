# Coding tasks: Session 2

1. **Easy.** Run `simulate(3, 10, 6, 3, 0.9, 0.5)` and print the hourly state of charge. At what hour is the battery empty and at what hour is it full?
2. **Stretch.** Change `cheapest_design` to use a battery that costs 800 per kWh instead of 400. Predict whether the best battery size goes up or down, then run it.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why the battery cannot reduce the import in the early morning of the dull day below 0.62 kWh for the 3 kW / 6 kWh design, even though it is large enough for the evening.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
