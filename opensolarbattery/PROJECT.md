# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opensolarbattery

**Short description:** Learn to size a rooftop solar array and battery: energy balance, state of charge, reliability and cost, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opensolarbattery is a beginner-friendly learning repo about one everyday engineering question: how big should a home's solar array and battery be? In three short sessions you go from one day of sunshine against one day of demand (surplus and deficit), to an hourly energy balance with battery power limits, efficiency and state of charge, and finally to a seeded 365-day simulation with random cloudiness, a loss-of-load reliability measure, validation against exact energy rules, sensitivity tests and a cost-optimal sizing search. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All costs are illustrative units, not market prices, and the model is educational, not an installation design. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as robust sizing over many weather years, smarter search and outage backup.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
