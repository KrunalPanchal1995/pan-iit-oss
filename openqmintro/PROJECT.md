# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openqmintro

**Short description:** Learn the basic ideas of quantum mechanics: superposition and interference, energy levels in a box and tunnelling, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openqmintro is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the first ideas of quantum mechanics. In three short sessions you go from the amplitudes of a two-state system and the interference of a single photon in an interferometer, to the allowed energies, the probability density and the emitted photons of a particle in a box, to tunnelling through a rectangular barrier, with the exact formula validated against a transfer-matrix solution that also handles smooth barriers, ending in an estimate of the sensitivity of a scanning tunnelling microscope. The models are deliberately small (two states, one dimension, ideal walls) so the numbers are illustrative, not data for a real device. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as the finite well, the time-dependent wave packet, the harmonic oscillator, the hydrogen atom and spin measurements.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
