# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opentolerance

**Short description:** Learn tolerances, ISO fits and tolerance stack-ups, from a pin in a hole to statistical simulation, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opentolerance is a beginner-friendly learning repo about a quiet but central idea in manufacturing: no part is made exactly, so every dimension carries a tolerance. In three short sessions you go from the limits of a pin and a hole, to the ISO hole-basis fit system (computing the clearance of a 25 H7/g6 fit and choosing fits for a sliding bearing and a press fit) and the worst-case stack-up of a chain of parts, to statistical stack-ups with root-sum-square, process capability (Cp, Cpk), Monte Carlo simulation checked against exact corner enumeration and the normal distribution, an illustrative cost model and a five-part tolerance allocation. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. Educational only: not a substitute for the standards or for professional design.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
