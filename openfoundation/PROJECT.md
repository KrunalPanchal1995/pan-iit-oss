# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openfoundation

**Short description:** Learn foundations and soil bearing: footing size, Terzaghi capacity, settlement and consolidation, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openfoundation is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the ground under a building. In three short sessions you go from the one idea that a weight must be spread over enough soil (pressure = force / area), to sizing a square footing for a 600 kN column with the footing's own weight, Terzaghi's bearing capacity, a factor of safety and the middle-third rule for off-centre loads, to settlement: elastic settlement, the slow consolidation of clay with its time curve, and how the load spreads with depth (the 2:1 rule against Boussinesq). Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models and must never be used to design a real foundation.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
