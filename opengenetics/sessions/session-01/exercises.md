# Coding tasks: Session 1

1. **Easy.** Change the cross in `lesson.py` to Aa x aa and print the counts. Predict them first.
2. **Stretch.** Write `chance_recessive(parent1, parent2)` that returns the probability of an aa child from the Punnett square and check it for all nine pairings.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain, with the square, why two parents who both show the dominant trait can have a child who shows the recessive trait but two aa parents cannot.

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
