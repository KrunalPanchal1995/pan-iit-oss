# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opengenetics

**Short description:** Learn Punnett squares, Hardy-Weinberg, chi-square, genetic drift and selection from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opengenetics is a beginner-friendly learning repo about how traits are inherited. In three short sessions you go from the Punnett square for one gene (3:1 appearance, 1:2:1 genotypes), to the probability rules, allele frequencies, Hardy-Weinberg proportions, a chi-square test of a 3:1 ratio and dihybrid crosses, to a seeded Wright-Fisher simulation of genetic drift, natural selection and mutation that you validate against formulas. The mini-project asks how large a conservation population must be to keep 90 percent of its heterozygosity for 50 generations. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All models are simplified textbook models for general education; nothing here is medical or genetic advice about any person. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show what to build next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
