# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openretainingwall

**Short description:** Learn earth pressure, sliding and overturning checks for a gravity retaining wall, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openretainingwall is a beginner-friendly learning repo about retaining walls. In three short sessions you go from the picture of soil leaning on a wall, to the Rankine earth pressure formula and its triangle of pressure, to the two stability checks used for a gravity wall: factor of safety against sliding and against overturning about the toe, plus the middle-third rule for the base pressure. You compare numerical integration with closed forms, test moment equilibrium of the whole wall, sweep width, friction and backfill slope, and see how water in the backfill drives the safety factors below 1. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. This is a simplified teaching model for learning only; it must never be used to design or check a real wall. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
