# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opennutrition

**Short description:** Learn energy balance: kilocalories, labels, resting energy and a body-mass model, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opennutrition is a beginner-friendly learning repo about energy balance: the energy in food against the energy the body spends. In three short sessions you go from a bank-balance picture with kilocalories and kilojoules, to calculating energy from grams of protein, carbohydrate and fat and estimating resting energy, to a dynamic model in which expenditure rises with body mass so that mass approaches a steady state. You compare the model with a closed-form answer, measure how much uncertain inputs blur the result with a Monte Carlo experiment, and study a failure case. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. This repo is general education only. It is not medical or dietary advice, it uses made-up people, and its formulas are population estimates with wide individual error. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
