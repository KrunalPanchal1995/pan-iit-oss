# Coding tasks: Session 2

1. **Easy.** Print the Mercator y of latitudes 10, 40 and 70 degrees with `mercator` and compare the gaps between them. Predict which gap is biggest.
2. **Stretch.** Use `mercator_inverse` to find the latitude that has y = 5000 km, and check it by sending the answer through `mercator`.
3. **Explain in your own words.** Explain why the Greenland and Africa boxes look equal on the Mercator map. Use the exaggeration at their middle latitudes (about 71 degrees and 1 degree).

Not sure where to start? Change one number in `lesson.py`, predict what happens, then run it.
