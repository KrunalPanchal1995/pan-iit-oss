# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openmapproject

**Short description:** Measure map distortion with the Jacobian and choose a projection for a country: from the orange peel to Tissot ellipses.

**Full description:** openmapproject is a beginner-friendly learning repo about maps of a round Earth. In three short sessions you go from the orange-peel puzzle and the simplest latitude-longitude map, to the Mercator projection and its area exaggeration (compare a box around Greenland with a box around Africa), to a finite-difference Jacobian that measures scale, angle error and area scale for four projections, validated against formulas and against the area of the sphere, 4 pi R^2. You also compare the shortest route (great circle) with the straight line on a Mercator map and see why Mercator fails at the poles. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo ends with a mini-project: choose and justify a projection for a country of your choice. It is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can go next, such as compromise world maps, polar maps and conformal conic maps.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
