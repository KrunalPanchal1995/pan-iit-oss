# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opengyro

**Short description:** Learn why a spinning top circles instead of falling: angular momentum, torque and precession, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opengyro is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the spinning top and the gyroscope. In three short sessions you go from the picture of a spinning wheel that resists being tilted (a torque arrow and an angular momentum arrow), to the formula for the precession rate and a design problem (choose the spin so a bicycle wheel circles once every 4 seconds), to a numerical simulation of the heavy symmetric top from the Lagrangian equations in Euler angles. You validate it with energy and the two conserved angular momenta, compare the simulated precession with the formula, measure the wobble (nutation), check the classical sleeping-top bound and see a top that falls over. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as a 3D view without Euler-angle singularities, friction at the tip and a spin-down.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
