# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openuncertainty

**Short description:** Learn how to carry measurement error through a formula and report an honest answer, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openuncertainty is a beginner-friendly learning repo about a question every lab report must answer: how sure are you of the final number? In three short sessions you go from a ruler reading of 12.3 +- 0.1 cm and the area of a table, to the standard error of the mean, the first-order propagation formula, error budgets and correlated inputs, and finally to Monte Carlo propagation, where you see the linear formula agree for small errors and fail for a ratio and for a function at its maximum. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can go next, such as sampling methods, sensitivity indices and surrogate models.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
