openphtitration

Find an unknown acid concentration from a titration curve, in three sessions.

  • Session 1: Acids, bases and the shape of a titration curve (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: pH, pOH and the mathematics of the strong acid - strong base curve (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: Weak acids: buffers, the numerical curve and the noisy endpoint (Level 3, 90 min)
