# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openphtitration

**Short description:** Learn pH, titration curves, buffers and indicators, and find a vinegar concentration from synthetic titration data.

**Full description:** openphtitration is a beginner-friendly learning repo about acids, bases and pH, built around one end goal: finding an unknown acid concentration from a titration curve. In three short sessions you go from the pH scale and the shape of a strong acid - strong base curve, to the mathematics (pH, pOH, the mole balance at equivalence, indicator choice and dilution), to a numerical weak-acid titration computed by solving the charge balance with a bisection root finder you write yourself. You check it against the Henderson-Hasselbalch relation, the half-equivalence rule and the analytic buffer-capacity formula, add noise to the volume readings, find the equivalence point from the maximum of the first derivative, study a failure case (very dilute acids, where water matters) and finish with a mini-project: the concentration of a synthetic vinegar sample. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. It is a simulation: real acids and bases need supervision. The repo stops on purpose; a Target animation and a roadmap show where to take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
