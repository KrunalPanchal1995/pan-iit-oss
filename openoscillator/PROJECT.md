# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openoscillator

**Short description:** Pendulums, springs, damping and resonance: from the period formula to an RK4 simulation, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openoscillator is a beginner-friendly learning repo about things that swing. In three short sessions you go from measuring the period, amplitude and frequency of a pendulum and a mass on a spring, to the mathematics of simple harmonic motion, energy exchange and damping (including how to choose a length or a stiffness for a target period), to a numerical simulation of the real nonlinear pendulum with the Runge-Kutta 4 method. You check the simulation against exact results, see why the small-angle formula fails at large swings, watch explicit Euler gain energy it should not have, and draw the resonance curve of a driven oscillator and its quality factor Q. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as chaos, coupled pendulums and fitting real phone data. The models are simplified teaching models, not validated engineering tools.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
