# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openclimate

**Short description:** Learn the greenhouse effect with a simple energy-balance model: sunlight in, heat out, one absorbing layer, from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openclimate is a beginner-friendly learning repo about why greenhouse gases warm the Earth. In three short sessions you go from the energy balance of a bare planet (sunlight absorbed, S (1 - albedo) / 4, against heat radiated, sigma T^4), which leaves the Earth 33 K too cold, to a one-layer atmosphere that lets sunlight through but absorbs infrared and explains the real 288 K surface, to a step-by-step simulation in which an ocean layer warms under rising CO2. You validate the simulation against exact answers and energy conservation, meet climate sensitivity and the warming still "in the pipeline", and find the CO2 level that keeps long-term warming below 2 K. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target and a roadmap show where the community can take it next. All models are simplified teaching models, not climate projections.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
