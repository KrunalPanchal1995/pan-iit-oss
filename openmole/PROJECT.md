# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openmole

**Short description:** Learn to balance equations and compute grams, moles and limiting reagents: stoichiometry from scratch in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openmole is a beginner-friendly learning repo about stoichiometry: the arithmetic that tells a chemist or a process engineer how much of each substance goes in and comes out. In three short sessions you go from the idea that atoms are conserved (so balancing an equation is bookkeeping), to molar masses, moles, limiting reagents, yield and concentration with a worked limestone design problem, to an automatic equation balancer that finds the integer coefficients as the null space of an element-by-species matrix. You validate it on known reactions, check mass and charge, sweep reagent feed ratios, test sensitivity to the atomic-mass table, meet the cases that cannot be balanced uniquely, and finish with a mini-project: how much hydrogen does one tonne of ammonia need? Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as reactions with several solutions, recycle loops and equilibrium.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
