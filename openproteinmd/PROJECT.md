# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openproteinmd

**Short description:** Learn molecular dynamics: atoms on springs, velocity Verlet, and a toy bead-chain peptide that folds. 3 sessions, numpy only.

**Full description:** openproteinmd is a beginner-friendly learning repo about molecular dynamics, the method scientists use to follow atoms with a computer. In three short sessions you go from two atoms joined by a spring and a Lennard-Jones pair, to a velocity-Verlet integrator whose energy conservation and time-step limit you measure against an exact answer, to a coarse-grained 12-bead hydrophobic/polar chain with a Langevin thermostat that collapses into a compact fold when it is cold. You validate the simulation with energy conservation (constant energy) and the equipartition thermometer, study its sensitivity to temperature and time step, and end with a mini-project: find the temperature below which the chain folds. Everything is a toy model in reduced units, not a real protein and not a tool for any medical or drug decision; it is general education only. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
