# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openportfolio

**Short description:** Learn how diversification lowers risk: correlation, portfolios and the efficient frontier, from scratch in 3 sessions (made-up data, not investment advice).

**Full description:** openportfolio is a beginner-friendly learning repo about portfolio risk. In three short sessions you go from two shops (umbrellas and ice cream) whose incomes cancel each other, to the mean, risk and correlation of two synthetic assets with a minimum-risk weight you derive and verify, to many assets: the limit that diversification cannot pass, the efficient frontier of four made-up assets, the Sharpe ratio, and the surprising ways estimated portfolios fail. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment) and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. Everything is general education only: the assets are synthetic with made-up returns, nothing here is investment advice, and models like this do not predict real markets. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as shrinkage estimates, stress tests and fat-tailed returns.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
